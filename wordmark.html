Target Air wordmark

Three directions, each shown large and then at the sizes that actually matter: 28px for the dashboard nav and 16px for the smallest useful case. Set in Poppins, the same face as the rest of the work. The accent is an ice blue chosen so it never collides with the red, amber and green the dashboard needs for blocked, at risk and on track.
